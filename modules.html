Learning Modules

Six modules covering the social engineering attacks that target remote workers most often. Take them in any order. Nothing is locked, and nothing requires an account.

Phishing

A fake message that impersonates a trusted organisation to steal your credentials, your money, or your access.

What you will learn

  • How email phishing, fake login pages, and QR phishing differ
  • The six signals that expose a phishing message
  • What to do in the first ten minutes after you click

Spear Phishing

Phishing written specifically for you, using real details gathered from your public professional footprint.

What you will learn

  • How attackers research a freelancer before contacting them
  • Why accurate personal details are not proof of legitimacy
  • The fake recruiter and fake client patterns to expect

Smishing

Phishing delivered by SMS, where a short message and a shortened link do the work of a whole fake website.

What you will learn

  • The three lures behind almost every scam text
  • Why SMS sender IDs and short links cannot be trusted
  • How to verify a delivery or bank alert safely
Reading materials Video 10-item quiz

Vishing

A scam phone call that uses live pressure to make you act before you have time to verify anything.

What you will learn

  • Why a live voice defeats caution that email would not
  • The OTP rule that stops nearly every vishing call
  • How AI voice cloning changes the callback rule

Pretexting

What you will learn

  • How a pretext is constructed across multiple messages
  • Why changed bank details are always a stop signal
  • The verification habit that breaks the whole attack
