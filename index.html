Learning modules

Six modules. Start anywhere.

Each module explains one attack type, shows you a realistic example, gives you a checklist of red flags, and tells you what to do if it happens to you. Five of them end with a ten-question quiz that is randomised every time you take it.

Phishing

Fake emails, cloned login pages, and QR codes designed to capture your password or one-time PIN.

Ask anything

Not sure about a message you just received?

Open the assistant in the bottom-right corner of any page and describe what you got. It will tell you which attack pattern it matches, what to look for, and what to do next, in plain language, without asking who you are.

  • “I got a text about a package I need to pay for. Is it safe to click?”
  • “Someone called saying they are from IT and asked for my OTP.”
  • “A new client wants my bank details before we sign anything.”
